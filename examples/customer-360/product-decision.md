# Product decision

**Decision:** extend Customer 360; do not create a duplicate churn-risk product.

The portfolio board concluded that the existing reusable customer profile is the correct product boundary. It funds the service-interaction extension, names the Customer Data Products team as accountable owner, and schedules a value review six months after adoption. Orders and Loyalty remain related input products rather than being copied into a new data product.

The decision is framework-level portfolio evidence. It is not an ODPS field.
