# Customer 360 objective and use case

This example is synthetic and exists to show the framework chain. The business objective is to reduce annual customer churn from 18% to 14% by the end of 2027. The retention director owns the target and the finance-approved churn report is the baseline source.

The use case is to identify customers at high risk of leaving within 30 days so approved retention interventions can be targeted. Recent purchases, service interactions, loyalty status and customer profile are required. Existing Customer 360, Orders and Loyalty products are searched first; the recorded gap is insufficient service-interaction capability.
