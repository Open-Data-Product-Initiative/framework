# Value review

Six months after release, the retention analytics group has adopted Customer 360 version 2.4 in the registered churn-risk workflow. Annual churn has moved from 18% to 17.2%, short of the 14% target. The review records other retention campaigns, seasonal changes and the observed completeness failure, so it treats Customer 360 as a possible contributor rather than the sole cause.

The board compares the extension's operating cost, remediation cost and observed use-case outcome before deciding whether to continue, expand or change the investment. This document is an example of framework-level value evidence, not a claim that ODPS defines a value-review field.
