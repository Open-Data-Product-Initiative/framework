# Enrichment Review Checklist

Before accepting any generated proposal, a maintainer checks:

- the problem is real and belongs in the stated target
- every material claim is supported by the cited source passage
- source authority and jurisdiction fit the claim
- limitations, conflicts and uncertainty are visible
- terminology matches the glossary and canonical capability names
- the proposal preserves the Core/profile distinction
- ODPS-family specifications are referenced rather than redefined
- requirements do not become vendor-specific
- machine-readable and human-readable concerns remain distinct
- no quotation or redistribution exceeds applicable rights
- model output has not been mistaken for source evidence
- assessment or conformance implications are handled explicitly

The reviewer records accept, revise or reject and provides a reason. Acceptance means that a human may make a deliberate Markdown edit; it does not promote the generated artifact itself to normative status.
