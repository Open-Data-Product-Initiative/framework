# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Data Product Operating Framework project is committed to a professional, inclusive and evidence-led collaboration environment.

Project participants must:

- engage respectfully with people and ideas
- critique proposals without attacking contributors
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest
- distinguish evidence, inference and opinion
- respect confidentiality, intellectual property and contribution provenance
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation and disruptive conduct

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, events and other spaces where a participant represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Conduct concerns should be reported privately to the maintainers through the contact method published by the Open Data Product Initiative. Do not publish sensitive reports in a public issue.

Maintainers may correct, hide or remove content; close contributions; restrict participation; or escalate a matter to the Initiative's governance body. Decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where necessary and consistent with applicable Initiative policies.
